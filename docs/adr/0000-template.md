# NNNN. Short slug-shaped title

- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Supersedes:** none
- **Superseded by:** none

## Context

What forced a decision. The constraint, not the conclusion — what was pressing,
what was not working, what two things could not both be true.

Write what was actually known at the time, including the guesses. Do not write
the answer here.

## Decision

What was chosen, plainly. One decision per file, in the imperative.

## Rejected options

**This section is the whole point of the file.** A decision with no rejected
options reads like a preference; with them, it reads like engineering.

One entry per option seriously considered, each with the specific reason it lost:

- **Option** — why not. Be concrete. "It was slower" is not a reason; "it added a
  network hop to every approval" is.

If you genuinely considered nothing else, say so and say why — that is itself
worth knowing later.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, and what it makes hard or impossible later. The second half
matters more.

Include what would have to change for this to be worth revisiting.
